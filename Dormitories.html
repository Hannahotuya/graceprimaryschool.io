Boarding section

For the boarders, efforts have been made to make their stay at Grace Primary School comfortable and condusive for learning.

The students have their own dormitories grouped according to different classes with matrons to oversee activities and help students in their duties for example; washing uniforms, cleaning up their rooms and also keeping their own personal property.Such women are akin to mothers and so much assurance is offered to parents knowing that their children are in safe hands.
